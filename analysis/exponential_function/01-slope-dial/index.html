Honesty · what we are assuming

That this limit exists for every is asserted here, not proved. It is provable — the usual route bounds by monotonicity arguments and squeezes — but no such proof appears on this page. Every single thing downstream rests on that one assumption.

What the table does show is that the quantity is numerically stable and reproducible to as many digits as you care to demand. That is evidence, not proof.
