The Factorial Conveyor

A function that is its own derivative is a machine whose output is unchanged by the act of differentiating it. Here we build one out of parts — a row of blocks on a belt — and discover that the parts were never ours to choose. The factorials are forced.

Every term here differentiates into the term to its left, exactly and without residue: . The that falls out of the power cancels the in the factorial. That cancellation is the gear ratio of the whole machine — and it is the only ratio that works.

Differentiating the whole series therefore shifts the belt one slot left. The constant at the front falls off into nothing; an identical block arrives from the infinite tail behind. The belt is unchanged. That is what looks like as a mechanism.

    Two moves above are licensed, not free — read what we assumed

    1. Term-by-term differentiation is a theorem. Writing is not a definition and not bookkeeping — it is a genuine result: a power series may be differentiated term by term at every point strictly inside its radius of convergence, because it converges uniformly on compact subsets there. We use this move and do not prove it. Note the mild circularity risk we avoid by ordering things correctly: in §5 we prove the radius of convergence is first, from the ratio test alone, so the licence is in hand before we differentiate.

    2. Matching coefficients assumes uniqueness of power series. From we concluded for each . That needs the identity theorem: a power series that vanishes on a neighbourhood of 0 has all coefficients zero (differentiate times at 0 and read off ). True, standard, and assumed here.

    3. And we assumed a power series exists at all. Strictly, §1 shows: if a solution is analytic at 0, its coefficients must be . The converse direction — that the resulting series really does converge and really does solve — is what §5 establishes. Together they close the loop.

    8 the essence

    Rate equals current amount — everything else is a consequence

    We asked for a single self-referential property: the speed at every instant equals the position at that instant. We did not choose a base, a number, or a growth rate. From that one demand:

    — the coefficients had to be , because leaves no alternative;
    — the sum had to be infinite, because differentiation always eats the top term;
    — the series had to converge everywhere, because factorials eventually beat any power;
    — the number is simply the reading at .

    The factorial is not decoration and not a mnemonic. It is the gear ratio that makes a shift lossless — the only weighting under which each term differentiates exactly onto its neighbour.

    The one-line summary

    Left end and right end are the same series. Re-indexing is the whole proof, and the belt is the picture of the re-indexing.
