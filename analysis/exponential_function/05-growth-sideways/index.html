The exponential function · approach 05

Growth turned sideways

One law: — your speed is your position, scaled and turned. Point the rate outward and you get explosive growth. Turn the same rate 90° and the identical law draws a circle. Growth and rotation are not two phenomena. They are one law with the rate aimed in different directions.

Three particles, all released from , all obeying . The only difference is where points. Curves are the exact solution ; the arrow is the live velocity.
The claim this page has to earn: is not a magic number that inherits its powers from. The function comes first — it is the unique motion whose velocity equals its position — and the number is a byproduct, one sample of it. Once you accept the function as primary, nothing stops you from aiming its rate sideways, and stops being mysticism and becomes a statement about travel time: half a turn.

With this is the function every course calls : the fixed point of differentiation, the one function that survives untouched. Sample it at and out drops the number — a consequence, not a premise.

Now here is the crack in the floor. Nothing in the sentence “velocity equals position, scaled by ” requires to be a real number. Positions can be points in a plane. Velocities can be arrows in a plane. And in the plane, multiplication by a number does something that has no analogue on the line: it can turn.
