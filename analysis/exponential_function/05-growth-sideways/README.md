# 05 — Growth turned sideways

**The same law draws a circle.** An interactive, fully offline explainer for the exponential
function, told through the complex plane: `z' = k·z` with a draggable complex rate `k`.

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step, no server, no network at view time.

---

## The idea

Every other route to `exp` treats the number `e` as the hero and the function as its
consequence. This page inverts that. The hero is the **law**:

> your velocity is your position, scaled by `|k|` and turned by `arg k`.

On the real line the only turns available are 0° and 180°, so the law can only produce
growth and decay. In the plane it can turn by *any* angle — and when the rate is turned a
full 90° (`k = i`), the velocity is permanently perpendicular to the position. A
perpendicular push cannot change your distance from the origin. It can only rotate you.

That single observation is Euler's formula. `e^{iπ} = −1` stops being mysticism and becomes
a statement about travel time: **half a turn.**

## The mathematics (all computed in-page, nothing faked)

- **Exact solution.** Sections 01, 03, 04 and 06 evaluate the closed form
  `z(t) = e^{at}(cos bt + i sin bt)` directly each frame. They are *not* simulations — this
  is stated on the page, in §07.
- **Velocity decomposition.** `kz = a·z + b·(iz)`. The real part of `k` is the radial push,
  the imaginary part is the sideways push. The page draws both components and shows
  `d|z|/dt = a·|z|` going to exactly zero as `a → 0`.
- **Arc length.** `∫|z'| dt = |k|·(e^{at} − 1)/a`, and `= |k|·t` in the limit `a = 0`. With
  `k = i` this is `t`, so arc = angle = time.
- **The series.** Terms are generated by `vₙ = vₙ₋₁ · (iθ/n)` — literally "rotate 90°, scale
  by θ/n" — and summed head to tail. Partial sums, the residual `|S_N − e^{iθ}|`, and the
  longest term `θⁿ/n!` are all live. At `θ = π, N = 20` the residual is `5.3e−10`, matching
  the first omitted term `π²¹/21! ≈ 5.7e−10`.
- **Integrators.** Forward Euler and classical RK4 are actually stepped over `[0, 2π]`. The
  readouts match the closed forms the prose asserts:
  - Euler: `|z_N| = (1+h²)^{N/2}` — verified, e.g. `N = 24 → 2.21556319`.
  - RK4: `|z_N| = (1 − h⁶/72 + h⁸/576)^{N/2}` — verified, e.g. `N = 24 → 0.99994680`.

## Honesty caveats surfaced in the UI

- `e^{iθ}` is **not** repeated multiplication. `exp` is *defined* on ℂ by the power series
  (equivalently by the ODE); the exponent notation is licensed only by
  `exp(z+w) = exp(z)exp(w)` surviving the extension. Said explicitly in §04.
- Splitting the series into `cos` and `sin` requires **absolute** convergence — Riemann
  rearrangement means this is not free. Named in §05.
- §07 is the only section that integrates anything, and it exists precisely to show that a
  naive integrator *cannot* stay on the circle: every Euler step is a hypotenuse.
- The velocity arrow is drawn 1:1 in the plane's own units, which makes it exactly one Euler
  step of length `Δt = 1` — the link between §03 and §07.
- The locked 3D views sit 120 units away with a ~2.6° field of view, i.e. effectively
  orthographic, so the "collapse" to a circle/wave is the real shadow and not perspective.

## Libraries (all vendored locally in `lib/`)

| Library | Version | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| **Two.js** | 0.8.19 | Retained-mode 2D scene graph for the seven complex-plane scenes. Trails, arrow-heads, dashed circles and tick labels are all persistent objects whose vertices are mutated per frame — cheaper and tidier than clearing an immediate-mode canvas seven times. Canvas renderer for long polylines. |
| **Three.js** | 0.149.0 | The §06 helix: `(t, cos t, sin t)` with its three shadows. UMD `three.min.js` — ES-module builds fail from `file://`. |
| **OrbitControls** | 0.147.0 | Globalised `THREE.OrbitControls`. Taken from r147 because `examples/js/` was deleted in r148; it is API-compatible with r149. |
| **anime.js** | 3.2.2 | Short user-initiated transitions: preset `k` moves, θ→π landmark scrubs, and the 900 ms camera/FOV flights between the three helix seats. |
| **KaTeX** | 0.16.11 | Every statement on screen. `.woff2` fonts vendored; the CSS was stripped of its `.woff`/`.ttf` fallbacks so nothing 404s offline. |

Deliberately *not* used: D3, decimal.js, Paper.js, math.js, GSAP, ECharts, p5.js, Chart.js,
big.js (each is the signature of another artifact in this set). Complex arithmetic is ~30
lines of plain JS — a numeric library would have added weight without adding a single digit
of accuracy at double precision.

## Gotchas hit while building

- **anime.js suspends its rAF engine when `document.hidden`** (`suspendWhenDocumentHidden`
  defaults to `true`). Set to `false` at boot, otherwise every transition silently freezes in
  a background tab.
- **Two.js `makePath()` re-centres the path** it creates. Used `new Two.Path(anchors, …)` +
  `two.add()` instead, so vertices stay in absolute pixel coordinates.
- **KaTeX refuses to render in quirks mode** — hence the leading `<!DOCTYPE html>`.
- **Whitespace between inline-flex children is dropped**, which glued the legend labels to
  their KaTeX spans. Fixed with `gap` rather than `&nbsp;`.
- Long display equations are wrapped in `overflow-x:auto` containers, and the two worst
  offenders were rebuilt with `\begin{aligned}` so they fit their panels.
